AI STRATEGY FACTORY - PRICING
Machine-readable pricing for AI agents and humans.
Markdown copy: /pricing.md  |  Last updated: 2026-06-29  |  Currency: USD

AI Strategy Factory is open-source (MIT) and self-hosted. The software is free.
You pay only for the third-party AI APIs it calls (Perplexity for research,
Google Gemini for synthesis), billed directly to your own API keys. Optional
managed consulting engagements are also available.

PLANS
- Open Source (Self-Hosted): $0/mo. Full pipeline, 15 strategy documents,
  2 PPTX decks, 2 DOCX reports, architecture diagrams, web UI + CLI.
  Limit: unlimited; you supply your own API keys.
- Pay-as-you-go, Quick analysis: ~$0.05 per company. One full strategy package,
  9 research queries, 2-3 minute turnaround.
- Pay-as-you-go, Comprehensive analysis: ~$0.50 per company. One full strategy
  package, 18 research queries, deeper research, 5-10 minute turnaround.
- Consulting (SOW): from $35,000. Done-for-you discovery, strategy,
  implementation support, and training. Scoped per engagement.

Note: The Quick and Comprehensive prices are estimated pass-through API costs,
not a fee paid to AI Strategy Factory. There is no subscription or seat license.

PER-ANALYSIS COST BREAKDOWN
- Quick:         9 research queries,  ~$0.05 per company, 2-3 minutes.
- Comprehensive: 18 research queries, ~$0.50 per company, 5-10 minutes.

Underlying Perplexity rates (per 1K tokens, input/output):
- sonar:                $0.001 / $0.001
- sonar-pro:            $0.003 / $0.015
- sonar-reasoning:      $0.001 / $0.005
- sonar-reasoning-pro:  $0.002 / $0.008
- sonar-deep-research:  $0.002 / $0.008
Gemini synthesis uses gemini-2.5-flash. Actual cost varies with company size and
output length; the per-company figures above are typical real-world totals.

CONSULTING (STATEMENT OF WORK)
Fixed-scope engagements priced by company size:
- Small      (<100 employees):       $35,000
- Medium     (100-500 employees):    $52,500
- Large      (500-2,000 employees):  $70,000
- Enterprise (2,000+ employees):     Custom quote
Base scope (Small): Discovery $5,000, Strategy $10,000,
Implementation Support $15,000, Training $5,000. Medium and Large apply
1.5x and 2.0x multipliers to the $35,000 base.

OVERAGE
No overage fees from AI Strategy Factory. You run it yourself with your own API
keys, so every analysis is billed by Perplexity and Google at the same
per-company rates. The software cost stays $0 whether you run 1 analysis or 10,000.

BILLING
- Software: $0. No subscription, no monthly fee, no per-seat license (MIT).
- API usage: Billed directly to you by Perplexity and Google Gemini,
  pay-as-you-go. No annual commitment; no monthly minimum.
- Consulting (SOW): Invoiced per engagement against the agreed statement of work.

FAQ
Q: Is the software really free?
A: Yes. MIT-licensed and self-hosted. You only pay the AI providers for the
   research and synthesis calls each analysis makes.

Q: What does a single company analysis cost?
A: About $0.05 in Quick mode and about $0.50 in Comprehensive mode, paid to the
   API providers - not to us.

Q: Do I need a subscription or credit card with AI Strategy Factory?
A: No. You bring your own Perplexity and Gemini API keys and are billed by those
   providers directly.

Q: Can you produce the strategy for us instead of us running it?
A: Yes - managed consulting engagements start at $35,000 and scale by company
   size (see the Consulting section above).
